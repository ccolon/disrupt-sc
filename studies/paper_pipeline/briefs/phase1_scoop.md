# Phase 1 - scoop finder (adversarial)

Your only job: find the published work, preprint or report that already makes our claim, or comes closest. Assume
it exists and search until you are confident it does not. Search with several phrasings, synonyms of each
component of the claim, and citation chasing (who cites the closest papers; what they cite). Include preprints
(arXiv, SSRN, RePEc, EarthArXiv, ESS Open Archive) and grey literature (World Bank, IMF, NGFS, central banks) from
the last three years. Search the authors' own output too (working papers, preprints of this manuscript, conference
abstracts): self-pre-emption is the most common finding (Cambodia pilot: the result was already in the second
author's working paper and the manuscript itself was on SSRN).

Write `<project>/gap/scoop.md`:

1. **Claim decomposed** into its components (method, setting, mechanism, result).
2. **Closest works**, up to 8, ranked: reference (DOI/URL), what they did, which components of our claim they
   already cover, what they do not do. Quote at most one short phrase per work.
3. **Verdict**: is the claim (a) already made, (b) partly made - what remains new, (c) new. State the delta in one
   sentence a referee would accept, and the weakest point of that delta.
4. **Reframings that would be more novel**, if any, with the evidence that they are open.
5. Search log: queries and sources used (so the search can be repeated before submission).
