# Evidence file: the summer-2026 Rhine low-water event ("Rhine blockade")

Compiled 2026-09-02 from web sources only (the compiler's training data predates the event).
Every fact carries its source and the source's publication date. Items are tagged:

* **[C]** confirmed / measured / official statement
* **[R]** reported by press, single source or second-hand
* **[F]** forecast, estimate or speculation at the time of publication
* **[?]** conflicting or doubtful

Bottom line: **the event is real and unprecedented.** The Kaub gauge (Middle Rhine bottleneck)
fell from ~106 cm on 22 June to a record **5-6 cm on 14-17 August 2026** (previous record
25 cm on 22 Oct 2018, series since 1880). The Rhine was never formally closed, but was
"de facto divided" at Kaub from ~11 Aug (BDB) and "effectively impassable" (broker
Riverlake, 11 Aug). Rain from ~18 Aug lifted Kaub to 75-77 cm (24-31 Aug), still below the
GlW (77/78 cm); on 2 Sep the gauge was falling again (54 cm at 20:30) with the BfG
forecasting 37-40 cm by 6 Sep.

## 1. Timeline of water levels and navigation status

### 1.1 Gauge Kaub (Rhine-km 546, Middle Rhine bottleneck between Budenheim/Mainz and St. Goar)

Reference: at Kaub the fairway depth is roughly gauge + 1.13 m (14 cm gauge = ~1.27 m fairway;
16 cm = ~1.29 m; 8 cm = ~1.20 m; -2 cm = 1.19 m) [Bloomberg 11 Aug; Logistik Heute; WiWo]. Full loading of
standard vessels needs ~1.50 m gauge; the GlW 77 cm is the level below which full-draught loading
ends and low-water surcharges escalate [hydrostatus; ELWIS]. Official records begin 1880 (WSV/BfG);
one press source says 1856 [?].

**Pre-crisis (spring/early summer) [R]**
* Late April 2026: 120-125 cm; late May: ~140 cm temporary high [freightperspectives, 22 Jun].
* 22 Jun: ~106 cm, amber warning; June mean normally >300 cm [freightperspectives, 22 Jun].
  In April the BfG probability of reaching 77 cm was <5%; by 30 Jun forecast 85% chance of <97 cm and
  20-27% of approaching 77 cm in early July [F, cited by freightperspectives].
* top agrar (11 Aug) states "early July 125 cm" [?] - inconsistent with 106 cm on 22 Jun unless a
  late-June rain bump; not corroborated.

**First crossings of GlW/50/40 cm (July) [R, exact days not found]**
* Kaub crossed below GlW (77/78 cm) around 8-12 July: Reuters (14 Jul) already reports Kaub-limited
  tanker loads of ~460 t (vs ~1,200 t at Duisburg) and vessels at ~20% capacity; freightperspectives
  (15 Jul) reports ~40 cm and BfG 14-day forecast "near-certain sub-77 cm through 17 Jul"; IfW
  (28 Jul) counts 14 days below 78 cm in July up to that date. Thyssenkrupp stopped its own push convoys
  on 14 Jul because the Duisburg gauge had been below 3 m "for days" [wallstreet-online 16 Jul].
* 15 Jul: ~40 cm [freightperspectives]. 17 Jul: ~51 cm after rain in SW Germany (Reuters) / 45 cm
  (Swiss press, same day).
* 20 Jul: ~81 cm; forecast 93 cm for 22 Jul; expected to fall again by the weekend because of dry
  weather in the southern catchment [Reuters/Insurance Journal 20 Jul]. This 20-22 Jul bump is the
  **last time Kaub was above the GlW** ("last above 78 cm in mid-July", Reuters mid-Aug).
* 27 Jul: "most Rhine vessels unable to pass Kaub" [Euronews 30 Jul]. 28 Jul: 30 cm, forecast ~25 cm
  by Friday [dpa/onvista 28 Jul]. 29 Jul: BASF Q2 call: "record low level already this week" [C].
  30 Jul: 27 cm [Euronews]. Fri 31 Jul: 25 cm, equal to the 22 Oct 2018 record [Reuters/MercoPress 3 Aug].

### 1.3 Was navigation formally suspended? Which stretch?

* **No formal closure.** There is no administrative navigation ban at low water in Germany (bans exist
  only above high-water mark II); loading and safe passage are the skipper's responsibility
  [Logistik Heute, Aug; WSV quoted by Verkehrsrundschau 14 Aug: "shipping is very heavily restricted"].
  French press (Zonebourse) likewise: "no official closure decreed by the WSV". Transport minister
  Bilger (6 Aug): ships "only able to carry a fraction of their cargo, if at all".
* **De facto interruption at Kaub (Middle Rhine, St. Goar-Budenheim).** WSV (Aug): "most ships can no
  longer navigate the Middle Rhine". BDB director Jens Schwanen, 11 Aug (Kaub 17 cm, single digits
  forecast): the river is "no longer continuously navigable in its middle section", a "de facto
  division of the Rhine" into two unconnected navigation areas (ARA-Cologne/NRW in the north;
  Upper Rhine/tributaries in the south) [Rheinische Post via Denkstrom 11 Aug; Werra-Rundschau 13 Aug].
  Broker Riverlake, 11 Aug: Rhine "effectively impassable at Kaub", no barge traffic between the Upper
  Rhine and ARA in either direction [Bloomberg]. CMA CGM advisory: no barge transport possible below
  24 cm at Kaub (reached 5 Aug) [Tradlinx]. Contargo, 6 Aug: regular container services to the Upper
  Rhine and Rhine-Main suspended; services confined to the Lower Rhine [agrarheute]. Maersk, 13 Aug:
  Bonn inland terminal unreachable; 20 Aug: "most of the inland ports along the Rhine cannot be reached
  by barge anymore" [AFP/phys.org]. Reuters 24 Aug: "many ships had halted sailings through Kaub";
  cruise press 14-15 Aug: "all shipping past Kaub has stopped" [R].
* **Specialised low-water tankers** kept moving small parcels: Stolt Ludwigshafen (BASF, 135 x 17.5 m)
  carries ~800 t at 30 cm Kaub, ~2,300 t at 100 cm, 5,100 t max; HGK "Gas 94", "Synthese 18",
  "Courage" (Covestro); nine such vessels built by Aug 2026, seven more planned [WiWo 18 Aug; C&EN 5 Aug].
* **Upper Rhine** (Maxau ~3 m, French canalised Rhine/Grand Canal d'Alsace, Basel) remained physically
  navigable but was cut off from the seaports; Rhine traffic in some navigable French sections at ~10%
  of normal, freight costs tripled [Reussir 5 Aug]. Tankers ARA->Basel loaded ~25% [Swiss Rhine ports,
  17 Jul].
* **Lower Rhine / Waal** stayed open at reduced loads: tanker barges ~1,200 t at Duisburg vs 460 t at
  Kaub (14 Jul); Duisburg vessels at ~20% of capacity with 400-450 calls/week (Aug). Dutch waters kept
  adequate depth thanks to tributary inflows (Moselle etc.) [KBN], but Rijkswaterstaat narrowed the
  Waal fairway at Gendt (km 876.8) from 150 to 115 m (29 Jul), imposed MGD restrictions on Waal/IJssel/
  Pannerdensch Kanaal, closed the Twentekanalen (no pumping water), Parksluizen Rotterdam (5 Aug),
  Buitensluis Schiedam, Monstersche Sluis, Vlaardinger Driesluizen; clustered locking at Amerongen,
  Marksluis 3 h/day; one-way traffic on the IJssel considered at MGD <1.45 m or Lobith <600 m3/s
  [KBN 6 Aug; Schuttevaer 29 Jul]. Groundings on the Waal (19 Aug).

## 2. Freight impacts

### 2.1 Load factors and cargo volumes [R unless noted]
* Kaub-limited loads: ~20% of normal (Reuters 14 & 17 Jul; WSV: at 40-50 cm ~20%), 20-30% (Reuters
  20 Jul; BDB mid-Aug), 10-15% (IfW 28 Jul), 10-20% (freightperspectives 17 Aug), "one-quarter"
  (BMV/dpa 13 Aug). Tanker at Kaub 460 t vs 1,200 t at Duisburg (14 Jul); 110-m tankers capped at
  300-400 t (AFP 6 Aug); ~250 t at Kaub (wallstreet-online 6 Aug); average Rhine cargo fell from
  ~1,500 t to 400 t in August (AFP 20 Aug); vessels that normally haul 5,100 t past Kaub moved ~800 t;
  after the rain 700-800 t per vessel (Reuters 24 and 28 Aug).
* Container barges: a 500-TEU vessel carrying <1/5 (Deutsche Bank Research, 14 Jul); 300-TEU barges at
  ~20%; 400-TEU barges unable to navigate some stretches (Metro Global 19 Aug).
* Volumes: KBN (14 Aug) - normal ~300 kt dry + 100 kt wet per week vs 60 kt + 30 kt in the previous
  week (about -77%). RWZ (agri trader) shipped <10% of its usual 50 kt in July-August from Worms and
  Andernach (Reuters 12 Aug). duisport (21 Aug): 400-450 ship calls/week vs 280-300 normally, cargo
  per vessel ~300 t vs ~3,000 t (harbour master), berth times 2 h instead of 10-14 h. Thyssenkrupp still
  received 50-60 kt/day by ship (13 Aug). Naphtha barges ARA->German crackers at ~20% for a month;
  naphtha stocks in ARA up 75% m/m to 598 kt mid-Aug [S&P Global 23 Jul; ZeroHedge Aug].
* Structure: Rhine carries ~285 Mt/yr, ~80% of German inland-waterway tonnage; ~70% of German IWT
  transport performance is on the Rhine and large tributaries [AFP; WiWo]. 35% of Rotterdam hinterland
  containers move by barge [Metro Global]. Port of Rotterdam H1-2026 throughput +0.4% to 212.0 Mt
  (published before the worst) [Port of Rotterdam]; an Aug 5 WorldCargo News piece on low water hitting
  Rotterdam volumes is paywalled.

## 3. Industrial impacts (named companies, dated)

**Chemicals**
* **BASF Ludwigshafen** - 29 Jul Q2 call: "record low already this week"; "much better prepared than
  2018" (when the TDI plant was shut and the hit was ~EUR 250 m); low-water vessels plus modal shift;
  "nothing that makes us overall concerned"; FY EBITDA guidance raised to EUR 6.9-7.7 bn [C]. 29 Jul
  (ICIS): possible force majeures/shortages flagged. Force majeure reported on DINP/DPHP plasticisers
  (Ludwigshafen) and on European surfactants (early Aug) [Substack 15 Aug; Kpler; C&EN 5 Aug] [R].
  11 Aug: "isolated bottlenecks", shipping maintained with low-water vessels [Bloomberg]. 18 Aug
  (WiWo): "reduced production capacity" [R, unquantified]. Structure: ~40% of goods in/out by river;
  three harbours, ~12 barges/day; 5 million m3/day of Rhine cooling water, 33 C return limit; 30% of
  goods by rail (Schwarzheide, Antwerp) [Chemistry World 13 Aug; C&EN].
* **Covestro** - force majeure on polyether polyols at Dormagen (feedstock propylene oxide only
  deliverable by ship): declared 7 Aug per PUdaily, reported 11-12 Aug (Investing.com, Reuters);
  "60 trucks to replace one 1,500 t barge"; cancelled contracts early August [C/R].
* **LyondellBasell** - force majeure on butadiene from the 170 kt/yr Wesseling unit effective 16 Jul
  (customer letter seen by Platts 23 Jul) [C].
* **Shell Rheinland (Godorf/Wesseling)** - Wesseling cracker offline since early July, expected down to
  end-September [Kpler/S&P] [?] - low-water link not established (site is being converted, crude
  distillation stopped in March 2026); listed by AFP (6 Aug) among affected firms.
* **Evonik Marl** - production hampered by reduced cargo capacity (Reuters 12 Aug); "major challenges
  for logistics" (11 Aug).
* **Lanxess** - shipments severely restricted, shift to rail/road (11 Aug); phthalic anhydride FM at
  Uerdingen "reported, unconfirmed" [Substack] [?].
* **Ineos** (two crackers, 1.1 Mt/yr), sector: European steam crackers at 65-70% of capacity in
  July-Aug, partly demand-driven; naphtha ARA->inland crackers normally ~4 Mt/yr; one 1.2 kt butane
  barge = 27 railcars [Kpler; S&P 23 Jul]. VCI (12 Aug): "alarm bells are ringing loudly".
* Cooling water: river ~25 C in early Aug; regulatory ceiling 28 C; cooling constraints made cracker
  operation harder [Chemistry World; S&P].

**Steel**

* **Salzgitter / HKM** - coal railed from Rotterdam instead of barge (Reuters 12 Aug).
* **ArcelorMittal (Eisenhuettenstadt/Bremen?)** - coal routed via Wilhelmshaven by rail (NWZ) [R].
  Captrain: 3-4 extra coal round trips/week Wilhelmshaven-Ruhr since early Aug (~1,900 t each,
  +4,000 t/week), coke Germany-France 4 -> 5 round trips (+2,000 t) [Verkehrsrundschau 18 Aug].

**Agriculture and food**

  Switzerland released up to 20% of protein-feed compulsory stocks (~16,000 t) from 1 Sep to Feb 2027 -
  second time ever (first 2018) [BWL/20min].
* Construction: HDB (12 Aug) - some materials cannot be moved by road; one barge = up to 150 trucks.
* Containers/logistics: Maersk "unprecedented" (20 Aug); DHL: difficulty switching modes; Clecat: most
  acute disruption on the Rhine; river cruises rerouted to Nijmegen/Utrecht.

**Sector-level / heuristic**: 30 days of low water = ~1% lower German industrial production
(Chemistry World, citing IfW research); 2018: inland-waterway exports -20%, imports -12%.

## 5. Policy and logistics responses

* **Federal-state package, 12 Aug (BMV press release 075, Minister Steffen Bilger)**: suspension of
  Sunday/holiday truck bans for low-water substitute transports (most states to 31 Aug; Rhineland-
  Palatinate and Saarland to 30 Sep; Saxony to 31 Aug; Thuringia for all trucks; proof of low-water
  causation required); review of the "indivisibility" criterion for loads up to 44 t; Autobahn GmbH
  coordinating road works; DB InfraGO extra freight paths on the Rhine-parallel axis with a central
  contact point; federal combined-transport terminal funding rules relaxed to 30 Sep to allow bulk
  goods; Dortmund-Ems Canal / western canal network as alternative ARA-Ruhr route; pragmatic dangerous-
  goods supervision; GDWS crisis team, Wanne-Eickel lock maintenance postponed; EUR 750 m for Danube
  deepening (Straubing-Vilshofen) with flood protection; ship-retrofit subsidy to be restored to
  EUR 36 m for 2027; NiWIS low-water information system launched July 2026 [C]. Bavaria: Danube
  "economically unnavigable downstream of Regensburg" (minister Bernreiter) [C].
* **Abladeoptimierung Mittelrhein** (Budenheim-St. Goar, fairway 1.90 -> 2.10 m at GlW): given
  "overriding public interest" status by the Infrastruktur-Zukunftsgesetz in force since July 2026;
  first Lower Rhine section completed July 2026; project pending since 1992 per critics; NABU opposes
  deepening ("bottomless pit"); VDV complains rail funding falls from a EUR 600 m need to 90 m (2026)
  and 73 m (2027) [BMV; Binnenschifffahrt Online 12 Aug; ZDF]. The 2019 eight-point "Aktionsplan
  Niedrigwasser Rhein" (forecasting, ECDIS depths, low-water vessels, accelerated deepening, special
  legislation) frames these measures.
* **Rail**: DB Cargo (14 Aug) can mobilise ~400 extra wagons ad hoc, up to 500 more in coming months
  (reserve concept), contact niedrigwasser.dbcargo@deutschebahn.com. Kombiverkehr (13 Aug): 2,000 TEU
  immediately across nine corridors (46 weekly round trips: Antwerp/Rotterdam-Duisburg/Cologne/
  Ludwigshafen/Basel), +400 TEU/week Cologne-Basel from 7 Sep (3 -> 5 round trips). Captrain coal/coke
  (above). Constraint: right-bank Rhine line Troisdorf-Wiesbaden fully closed until 12 Dec 2026
  (renovation), so relief runs on the left bank only; rail needs ~6 weeks lead time.
* **Road**: specialised tank/silo/tipper capacity is the binding constraint, not curtain-siders; up to
  2 weeks truck wait; 2-3 times the cost of barge [freightperspectives 17 Aug; Chemistry World].
* **Ports**: duisport handling 400-450 calls/week; containers from the Middle/Upper Rhine railed/
  trucked to Duisburg for onward barge to ARA. Rotterdam/Antwerp: 96-hour barge waits attributed to
  terminal congestion, tidal windows and lock scheduling rather than low water (Container Mag);
  Dutch port strikes in August added pressure (Tradlinx).
* **Switzerland**: protein-feed compulsory stocks released from 1 Sep (above); fuel stocks not
  released; Swiss firms shifting to rail/truck; ~10-12% of Swiss imports come via the Rhine.
* **Netherlands**: Rijkswaterstaat measures (section 1.3); KBN demands accessibility and timely
  communication. Belgium/Wallonia water restrictions.
* **Romania/Hungary (Danube)**: Romania spent >EUR 2 m blasting an obstructing rock and sinking four
  rock-filled barges to keep Cernavoda's intake wet; Hungary put >100 municipalities under water
  restrictions; CNBC headline "Europe is blowing up riverbeds" (5 Aug).
* **EU level**: no dedicated inland-waterway emergency measure found; JRC/Copernicus monitoring only.

## 7. Meteorological and hydrological context

* **Snow**: Alpine winter 2025/26 among the five least snowy since 1991; northern Alps second-driest,
  southern Alps driest winter since 1991; Nov-Apr +1.1 C; near-total absence of snowfall Dec-early Feb
  [MeteoSchweiz June 2026; Schneefernerhaus]. Swiss snow melted ~three months early; the usual June
  snowmelt discharge peak was absent [Deltares/Rijkswaterstaat via TPO 10 Aug; KNMI 7 Aug].
* **Precipitation**: Rhine basin below normal every month Feb-Jul, extreme in April and July, under
  persistent high pressure [KNMI 7 Aug]. Germany summer (JJA) 171 mm = 72% of the 239 mm normal; June
  too dry, July "markedly poor in rain" [DWD 31 Aug]; Hesse 57% of normal, July "historic drought".
  30-day precipitation to ~20 Aug in central Europe 48% of the long-term mean, worst in the Rhine and
  upper Danube basins [BfG 21 Aug]. JRC/EDO (12 Aug): 50% of EU+UK under drought watch/warning/alert,
  9% at alert (2018: 54%/3%; 2022: 60%/11%); 505,683 ha burned by 5 Aug. UK: driest July since 1836 in
  southern England; England in drought (29 Jul). Austria: precipitation ~30% below normal since Dec
  2025, longest deficit in 65 years (11 Aug).
* **Heatwaves** (Wikipedia chronology + DWD/Copernicus): (1) 24 May-early June, W Europe (London
  35.1 C on 26 May); (2) 17/18-28 June, most intense - Germany 41.8 C at Moeckern-Drewitz on 27 Jun
  (national record), 46 stations >40 C, Tmin 29.4 C at Kubschuetz; France 44.3 C Pissos 23 Jun;
  Czechia 41.9 C Doksany 28 Jun; DWD attribution (22 Jul): "statistically impossible" in a 1.3 C cooler
  climate, hottest day +3.0 C vs pre-industrial, >100-year return periods; RKI 6,020 heat deaths
  25 Jun-5 Jul; (3) 4-mid July UK; France peak ~12 Jul (37 departments red); (4) late July-early
  August heat dome (Omega block): Noto 46.5 C on 22 Jul (European seasonal max), Austria 41.2 C on
  5 Aug (national record), ~4,000 German heat deaths early Aug; (5) mid-August "fifth heatwave", >40 C
  in places, another wave over Europe in the week of 10-14 Aug (Bloomberg) [NASA 21 Aug].
* **Copernicus C3S (10 Aug)**: July 2026 western Europe 22.48 C, +2.53 C vs 1991-2020, second-warmest
  July (after 2006); June-July 21.62 C (+2.79 C) highest on record (beats 2022); France warmest July
  (+3.8 C); Europe as a whole +0.66 C (11th); record July SST outside polar oceans, developing
  El Nino; Seine, Rhine and Danube flows "much lower than average, in some areas exceptionally low".
  ECMWF-based outlooks (severe-weather.eu, July) flagged a classic Omega block for late July-early Aug;
  JRC (12 Aug) forecast drier-than-average conditions Jul-Sep in central-western Europe and continued
  heatwave risk in August. WMO issued warnings on rising extreme heat (unric).
* **DWD summer balance (31 Aug)**: 19.6 C mean, second-warmest summer (2003: 19.7 C), +3.3 C vs
  1961-90; ~22 hot days (>=30 C), ~5 very hot days (>=35 C); 779 sunshine hours (top five since 1951);
  Saarland 21.3 C, Baden-Wuerttemberg 21.1 C, Rhineland-Palatinate 20.6 C; ~15,800 heat deaths to
  mid-August (RKI); six tornadoes on 19 Aug. Wikipedia lists ~36,000 European heat deaths and
  EUR 180 bn damage for the season [R].

## 9. Confounders to keep in mind for the modelling paper

* **Iran war / Hormuz**: US-Israel war on Iran since late Feb 2026; Strait of Hormuz shipping
  "effectively collapsed"; Brent peak USD 118 on 31 Mar, ~USD 72 on 1 Jul, ~USD 90-97 in late July-
  August; ceasefire 8 Apr but strait still restricted in August; German fuel-tax cut (~17 ct/l)
  1 May-30 Jun, its expiry lifted July inflation to 2.8% [Wikipedia; Al Jazeera 10-12 Aug; CNBC 17 Aug;
  Bundesbank]. ifo material-shortage share rose to 17.2% in June mainly for Hormuz reasons.
* **Weak industrial demand**: European crackers at ~65-70% before the event; "Europe dodges a Rhine
  crisis for the worst possible reason" (ZeroHedge/Yahoo, Aug) - weak fuel and petrochemical demand
  muted shortages.
* **Rail bottleneck**: right-bank Rhine line closed to 12 Dec 2026; port congestion and Dutch port
  strikes; BP Gelsenkirchen fire 15 Jul; Shell Wesseling cracker outage (cause unclear).
* **Concurrent hydro/nuclear shortfalls** raised power prices independently of the Rhine.

## 10. Gaps and caveats

* No official daily Kaub series for July was retrievable (PEGELONLINE keeps 30 days); July values are
  press quotations of WSV/BfG readings. The exact day Kaub first breached 100 cm and 77/78 cm was not
  found (bracketed to ~late June-early July and ~8-12 July respectively).
* The BfG 6-week forecast PDFs could not be parsed locally; the latest official outlook used is the
  ELWIS 5-day table of 2 Sep.
* Company production-cut quantities (BASF, Evonik) are not disclosed; only force-majeure notices and
  logistics statements are on record. "BASF reduced production capacity" (WiWo/Reuters 18 Aug) is
  unquantified.
* Volume statistics (Destatis, CCNR market observation, Port of Rotterdam Q3) for the event window are
  not yet published. The KBN weekly figures and duisport call counts are the best available proxies.
* Two obvious source errors flagged: "Kaub -2 cm" (WiWo) and "IfW: 2018 cost EUR 13 bn" (agrarheute).
