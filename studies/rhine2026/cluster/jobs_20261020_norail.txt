# Rhine 2026 cluster batch (20 Oct 2026): no rail alternative for bulk at all (the realism question of the user's review,
# 20 Oct). In the paper's rule a bulk shipment whose normal route already carries a main leg by rail (>= 100 km) may go
# around Kaub by rail at no penalty (the only bypass of the Kaub stretch in the network: 89 km of rail between Koblenz and
# Mainz); 15 % of the Kaub-crossing bulk orders have such a route, and the surcharge moves them off the river before the
# cut (21.8 bn USD of bulk over the 2026 run), which is why the surcharge LOWERS the loss (1.64 against 1.82 % with the
# capacity limit alone). We have no data on the share of the Kaub traffic that travels combined rail-barge, so this pair
# of runs bounds the effect: the line-haul threshold raised to 500 km makes every rail bypass a switch of mode, which is
# prohibitive for bulk, so no bulk reroutes (97 % of the cut bulk gives up in the offline classification, Table S-thresholds).
# line_haul_km lives under switching_costs, which the cache fingerprint ignores: launch with --independent on the shared
# caches of 2026_s07_base (the code needs the --line-haul-km flag of run_rhine.py, commit of 20 Oct).
#
#     bash studies/rhine2026/cluster/launch_rhine_batch.sh --jobs studies/rhine2026/cluster/jobs_20261020_norail.txt --independent
#     bash studies/rhine2026/cluster/pack_results.sh --jobs studies/rhine2026/cluster/jobs_20261020_norail.txt
#
