# Rhine 2026 cluster batch (29 Sep 2026): the wave-isolation experiment (review point NS4, test T5).
# The claim "a low-water season is priced by its calendar" rests on comparing the two troughs of 2026,
# which differ in length and depth. This batch isolates the memory of the stocks: wave A alone (weeks 1-10
# of the 2026 profile), wave B alone (weeks 11-19), A then B with 1, 2, 4 and 8 weeks of normal water between
# them (gap 0 is the 2026 profile itself = 2026_gs1_base, not rerun), and a flat profile with the same tonnage
# turned away spread evenly over 19 weeks. Profiles: build_wave_profiles.py -> scenarios/2026_wave*.csv.
# Statistic: I(g) = L(A, gap g, B) - L(A) - L(B) on the cumulated German loss (wave_isolation.py, from the
# batch table). Same representation and settings as the paper set: gate + surcharge, stocks x1, pipelined
# flows, seed 42, 20 recovery weeks after the last profile week, light export. Caches valid: independent jobs.
