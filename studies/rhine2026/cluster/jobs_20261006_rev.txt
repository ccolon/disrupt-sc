# Rhine 2026 cluster batch (6 Oct 2026): the sensitivities of the revision after the review of 5 Oct 2026.
# Same rules and profile as the batch of 30 Sep (the river as a quantity constraint with the voyage surcharge, stocks
# at their evidence values, crude and gas by pipeline, construction a non-storable input; the 2026 profile of 30 Sep).
# Blocks:
#   rail50 / rail140   bounded substitution (review OR2): up to 50 and 140 kt a week of cut bulk may switch to rail
#                      after the gate, pro rata (50 kt = DB Cargo's 400 wagons at short notice; 140 kt = the rail response
#                      of Ademmer et al., App. B, in a full low-water month). The paper's rule is a ceiling of zero.
#   head10 / head25    the normal-water headroom of the gate (review OR1): capacities anchored at the modelled baseline
#                      flow + 10 % and + 25 % instead of + 2 % (private caches: the capacity file is a build key).
#   rest15 / rest60    the refill time of a stock gap (review OR4): 15 and 60 days instead of 30.
#   floor02 / floor10  the loading table's floor below 5 cm (review NS1): 0.02 and 0.10 instead of 0.05.
#   deeplocal          the fairway lever on the Kaub reach alone (review NS3): +20 cm on its capacity and surcharge, the
#                      other reaches at the observed gauge.
#   fleetwide          the low-water fleet with its gain carried to 100 cm (review NS2; the Stolt Ludwigshafen loads
#                      2,300 t at 100 cm).
#   dfuel0             the power sector's fuel-oil input non-critical (review E4): C19 -> D set to 0 in the survey matrix.
#   serv45             a 45-day coping duration for the non-storable inputs instead of 90 (review E4).
#   q25 / q75          the outlook's 25th and 75th percentile paths as bounding profiles (review NS4; not trajectories).
