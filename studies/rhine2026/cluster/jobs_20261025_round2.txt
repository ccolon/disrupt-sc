# Rhine 2026 cluster batch (25 Oct 2026): RECALIBRATION ROUND 2, ten candidates on the TAGGED network.
# Round 1b (eight candidates on the untagged network) showed: (1) the container knob is a cliff: rate 0.015 + waterway value
# of time 0.15 put 110 Mt/yr of containers through Kaub (target ~10) and flooded every waterway (DE waterway tkm 167 bn vs 41.5);
# (2) at the Rhine's rate the canals, the Elbe and the Danube carry 3-10x their Destatis tonnage, while the Rhine itself is
# within the counts -> rates by waterway are needed (the `waterway` tag); (3) the Rotterdam/Amsterdam and Genoa/Venice splits
# turn on cost margins of cents per ton -> per-port fees (the `port_name` tag; logistics.loading_fees.by_port, code of 25 Oct);
# (4) rail +40 % abroad overshot (FR rail 1 % of tkm against 9) -> +20 %; (5) port fees on all ports collapse the short sea.

# (the network stage reads them through filepaths.transport / filepaths.multimodal), and pull
# the code (the by_port fee rule). Every run: private caches, two weeks of the smoke profile, no capacity gate, full export;
# score with onboarding/scripts/flow_score.py (the tags are carried into the export, so no on-the-fly tagging).
